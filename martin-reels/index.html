Martin Reels

by Martin Homola · Vibetesting

Martin Reels is a private automation tool that publishes short educational videos about software testing, ISTQB certification and AI to Martin Homola's own channels: YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and Facebook Page Reels.

What it does

  • Uploads each new short video to the owner's own YouTube channel (Google scope youtube.upload).
  • Reads basic statistics about the owner's own channel and videos (Google scope youtube.readonly).
  • Publishes the same video to the owner's Instagram professional account and Facebook Page.

It is used only by its owner. It does not access other people's accounts or collect data from the public.
