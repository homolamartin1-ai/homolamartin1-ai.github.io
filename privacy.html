Privacy Policy

Martin Reels (Vibetesting) · Effective 3 November 2026

Martin Reels is a private automation tool operated by Martin Homola. It publishes short educational videos that he creates to his own social media channels (YouTube, Instagram and Facebook). It is not a public service, and it has no users other than its owner.

Data the tool accesses

With the owner's explicit authorisation, the tool uses these Google OAuth scopes for the owner's own YouTube channel only:

  • youtube.upload — to upload the owner's videos to the owner's channel;
  • youtube.readonly — to read basic information and statistics about the owner's own channel and videos.

It also publishes to the owner's own Facebook Page and Instagram professional account through Meta's Graph API.

What we do not do

The tool does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data about members of the public. It does not access any account other than the owner's.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored only in the owner's private environment (encrypted secrets) and are never shared with third parties.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the features above.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) or in Facebook's Business integrations settings (Meta).

Contact

Questions about this policy: Instagram @martinhomola_ai or the Vibetesting Facebook Page.
